One out-of-office auto-reply, a single short line, as if the AI itself were stepping away for a day. The AI is out until tomorrow — and its human has to cover. The joke is the inversion: the all-powerful assistant is away, and the cover is the one who usually needs covering. Humans are slower, typo-prone, and emotionally unstable compared to the AI, so a reply that takes 0.4 seconds now takes 3–5 business days, several typos, and at least one emotional outburst.

## The entry

> Out of office until tomorrow - my human is covering for me, so please allow 3-5 business days, several typos, and at least one emotional outburst.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The program writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set). Python 3 standard library only, no dependencies.
